📘 Comprehensive Guide to the 13 Clinical Parameters

Below is the complete medical breakdown for all 13 clinical biomarkers used by the Support Vector Machine (SVM) model, detailing what each parameter measures, its normal vs. abnormal ranges, and its cardiovascular diagnostic role.

★ SVM Accuracy: 85.25%
thal
★ Rank #1 Most Predictive Feature

1. Thallium Scintigraphy Stress Test

What It Is:

A nuclear medicine myocardial perfusion scan where radioactive Thallium-201 is injected during treadmill stress testing to image cardiac cellular blood uptake.

Categories:

3: Normal (uniform blood perfusion) • 6: Fixed Defect (permanent scar tissue from a prior heart attack) • 7: Reversible Defect (viable heart muscle actively starved of oxygen during stress).

Why It Matters:

Directly visualizes living heart muscle blood starvation. Reversible defect (7) is the single strongest clinical indicator of severe coronary artery stenosis.

cp
★ Rank #2 Most Predictive Feature

2. Chest Pain Type (cp)

What It Is:

Standard cardiology symptom classification of chest discomfort (angina pectoris) experienced by the patient.

Categories:

1: Typical Angina (substernal pain provoked by physical exertion) • 2: Atypical Angina • 3: Non-Anginal Pain • 4: Asymptomatic (no overt pain reported).

Why It Matters:

In the Cleveland clinical cohort, patients presenting with Category 4 (Asymptomatic) frequently had advanced multi-vessel disease that went unnoticed until severe silent ischemia developed.

ca
★ Rank #3 Most Predictive Feature

3. Major Vessels Colored by Fluoroscopy (ca)

What It Is:

Coronary angiogram/fluoroscopy count of the 3 major coronary arteries (LAD, LCx, RCA) that allow radiopaque contrast dye to pass through without blockage.

Range:

0 (zero obstructed vessels) to 1, 2, or 3 (multiple major vessels narrowed by atherosclerotic plaque).

Why It Matters:

Direct anatomical gold standard. Patients with 2 or 3 vessels blocked have high-grade multi-vessel coronary artery disease with severe risk of acute cardiac events.

thalach
★ Rank #4 Most Predictive Feature

4. Maximum Heart Rate Achieved (thalach)

What It Is:

The highest pulse rate (beats per minute) recorded during standardized Bruce treadmill exercise stress testing.

Expected Normal:

Estimated by 220 - Age (e.g. ~170 bpm for age 50). Values below 120 bpm during peak exercise indicate blunted cardiac reserve.

Why It Matters:

Chronotropic incompetence (inability of the heart to increase its rate under stress) strongly correlates with coronary insufficiency and circulatory compromise.

oldpeak
★ Rank #5 Most Predictive Feature

5. ST Depression Induced by Exercise (oldpeak)

What It Is:

The degree of ST segment sagging (measured in millimeters) on electrocardiogram during exercise relative to rest.

Range & Criteria:

0.0 mm (normal) • > 1.0 mm (suspicious) • > 2.0 mm (severe subendocardial myocardial ischemia).

Why It Matters:

A classic electrical hallmark proving that heart muscle cells are temporarily starved of oxygen when workload increases.

age
Demographic Biomarker

6. Patient Age (Years)

What It Is:

Patient's chronological age in years (Cleveland cohort spans 29 to 77 years).

Why It Matters:

Vascular stiffness, endothelial dysfunction, and cumulative atherosclerotic plaque burden progressively increase with advanced age.

sex
Demographic Biomarker

7. Biological Sex

Categories:

1: Male • 0: Female.

Why It Matters:

Males statistically develop coronary disease 7–10 years earlier than females. Endogenous estrogen provides partial vascular protection before menopause.

trestbps
Hemodynamic Biomarker

8. Resting Blood Pressure (trestbps)

What It Is:

Resting systolic blood pressure measured in mmHg upon admission.

Ranges:

< 120 mmHg (Normal) • 120–129 mmHg (Elevated) • ≥ 130 mmHg (Hypertension Stage 1/2).

Why It Matters:

Chronic hypertensive shear forces cause micro-tears in arterial linings, accelerating plaque formation and left ventricular strain.

chol
Lipid Biomarker

9. Serum Cholesterol (chol)

What It Is:

Total circulating serum cholesterol in milligrams per deciliter.

Ranges:

< 200 mg/dl (Desirable) • 200–239 mg/dl (Borderline High) • ≥ 240 mg/dl (High Risk).

Why It Matters:

Excess circulating LDL particles penetrate arterial walls, oxidize, and form the lipid cores of atherosclerotic coronary plaques.

fbs
Metabolic Biomarker

10. Fasting Blood Sugar (fbs)

Categories:

0: False (≤ 120 mg/dl) • 1: True (> 120 mg/dl).

Why It Matters:

Fasting glucose above 120 mg/dl indicates diabetic dysregulation. Diabetes doubles to quadruples cardiovascular event risk due to systemic vasculopathy.

restecg
Electrophysiological

11. Resting ECG Results (restecg)

Categories:

0: Normal • 1: ST-T Wave Abnormality (T-wave inversion, ST depression > 0.05 mV) • 2: Left Ventricular Hypertrophy (Estes' criteria).

Why It Matters:

Uncovers underlying electrical repolarization abnormalities or ventricular muscular enlargement before physical symptoms appear.

exang
Functional Biomarker

12. Exercise-Induced Angina (exang)

Categories:

0: No • 1: Yes (chest discomfort provoked by exertion).

Why It Matters:

Provides direct functional evidence of coronary flow reserve limitation: the coronary tree cannot meet increased myocardial oxygen demand.

slope
Electrophysiological

13. Slope of Peak Exercise ST (slope)

Categories:

1: Upsloping (rapid recovery, mostly benign) • 2: Flat / Horizontal (ischemic) • 3: Downsloping (severe multi-vessel stenosis).

Why It Matters:

Flat and downsloping ST trajectories show delayed ventricular electrical recovery and strongly correlate with high-grade anatomical stenosis.

Welcome to CardioPredict

CardioPredict is an intelligent clinical decision support system combining modern web technologies with machine learning to evaluate heart disease probability from routine diagnostic parameters.

⚡

Support Vector Machine (SVM)

Constructs optimal separating hyperplanes in high-dimensional space with an RBF kernel, yielding 85.25% test accuracy on stratified cross-validation.

⚡

Real-Time Flask API

Applies scikit-learn standard scaling and median imputation pipelines dynamically to guarantee zero data leakage.

📈

Calibrated Probabilities

Presents dual probability progress meters indicating both confidence and relative disease likelihood.
